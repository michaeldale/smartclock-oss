# 0.1.1

Date: 2026-10-05

Status: Released 2026-10-06 (tag `v0.1.1`).

A small release on the 0.1.0 base. Its other purpose is the first update
through our own `/update_ota` on the device, which 0.1.0 has not yet run.

## Scope

1. **Daylight saving.** POSIX TZ rule, default Sydney, Timezone list on the
   web page ([issue](../issues/2026-10-05-clock-one-hour-behind-during-dst.md)).
   Done; active on the device.
2. **First update through our own updater.** Flash 0.1.1 from the clock's own
   Firmware Update card. Record the result as a decision record: upload,
   reboot, `/version` showing 0.1.1, settings kept. Done
   ([record](../decisions/2026-10-05-first-update-through-own-ota.md)).
3. **mDNS.** Explained: different subnet. Per-unit name and DHCP hostname added
   ([issue](../issues/2026-10-05-smartclock-local-not-resolving-on-windows.md)).
6. **Open-Meteo (OpenWeatherMap removed), reset-reason power cycles, heap gates**
   ([record](../decisions/2026-10-05-open-meteo-reset-reason-and-discovery.md)).
   In the checkout; next is the device.
4. **Settings page.** `web/index.html`, gzipped into flash, with `/status`
   and `/scanwifi?detail=1` behind it. Checked against a local mock, then flashed;
   the device serves it and `/status` reports live state.
5. **Clock faces.** All seven faces, forecast, photos and rotation set; checked
   on the PC with `tests/face_preview`. Next is the panel.

## Release checklist

- `pio run` passes the size guard; `python tests/run_safety_tests.py` passes.
- Device: correct local time, `/config` shows `tz`, `/status` not in recovery.
- Power-cycle recovery still enters and leaves recovery.
- Move the 0.1.1 section of [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md) from
  Unreleased to the date, set `FW_VERSION` to `0.1.1`, tag `v0.1.1`.

## Not in 0.1.1

Authentication ([issue](../issues/2026-10-05-ap-and-ota-unauthenticated.md))
and the remaining themes are in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).
